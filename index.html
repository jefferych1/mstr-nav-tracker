What changed, and what it did to the numbers above.

    Basic + converts + STRK conversions + options/RSUs
    These are the published figures. To correct one, edit data/overrides.json in the repository — the site picks it up about a minute later.

    Method. BTC NAV = bitcoin held × bitcoin price. Market-cap mNAV = (MSTR price × shares) ÷ BTC NAV. EV mNAV adds convertible-note principal and preferred notional to market cap and, when the toggle is on, nets the USD reserve, following Strategy's own presentation on strategy.com. "Assumed diluted" is Strategy's ADSO figure (basic plus all converts, STRK conversions, options and RSUs, regardless of moneyness).

    History. The series starts on 11 August 2020, the day of Strategy's first bitcoin purchase (21,454 BTC). Prices are actual closes — daily from StatMuse for Aug 2020–Sep 2021 and from IBKR since Sep 2025, weekly closes from IBKR in between; share counts are interpolated between year-end figures on strategy.com/shares, so the historical ratio is a close approximation rather than a filed number. Bitcoin holdings follow the announced purchase history.

    Refresh. A scheduled job runs every weekday after the US close, records MSTR's closing price and the bitcoin price, and appends a point to the history. A second job walks each new Strategy 8-K and updates bitcoin holdings, the USD reserve and the share count; the convertible and preferred balances move only at a 10-Q, which raises a flag at the top of this page rather than being scraped. The bitcoin price on this page also refreshes live in your browser, so the headline ratio moves with bitcoin between closes. Nothing here needs anyone to log in or approve anything.
